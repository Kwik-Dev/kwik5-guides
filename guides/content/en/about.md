## What Kwik is

Kwik Exporter, a UXP plugin that turns layer groups into pages; an editor for what those pages do; and a
runtime on [Solar2D](https://solar2d.com) that ships them to iOS, Android and desktop. The point has
never been "no code for its own sake" — it is that an illustrator should not need a developer to put
a book on a phone.

Two pieces are not ours. The foundation is [Solar2D](https://solar2d.com/): a mature, MIT-licensed 2D
engine, the official open-source fork of Corona SDK, maintained by its community after Corona Labs
stopped supporting the original. Kwik is a plugin and a pipeline on top of it — we do not ship a
private runtime, and a Solar2D book stays a Solar2D book. The second is the local generation backend,
[guaardvark](https://github.com/guaardvark/guaardvark), Albenze, Inc.'s MIT-licensed project: Kwik
forks it ([Kwik-Dev/guaardvark](https://github.com/Kwik-Dev/guaardvark)), tracks upstream, and credits
it as a fork everywhere the site names it.

Kwiksher was the first *Official Partner* of the Corona Labs partner program, which is the lineage
Solar2D continues.

## A short history, honestly

| when | what |
|---|---|
| May 2011 | Kwik 1.0 ships. |
| February 2012 | Kwiksher LLC is founded in Florida, by **Alex Souza**, who created the original plugin for designers, illustrators, and anyone who was not already a developer. |
| 2014 | Alex steps away; Naoya Yamamoto takes over in September. |
| 2026 | The exporter, editor and runtime become free and open source, and the paid thing becomes **cloud GPU rendering** — because that is the only part that genuinely costs money to run. See the decision log in the repository. |

## What we do not do

- No licence gate on the software, and no phone-home from local work.
- No royalties or revenue share on anything you make — you own your books outright.
- No training on your art. Not a policy we can revoke quietly; it is a decision with a number in the
  decision log.
- No showing your work in a showcase without asking first, and never without an explicit yes.
