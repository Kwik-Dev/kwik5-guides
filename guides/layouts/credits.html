Already bought credits?

  1. Find your key

    Your licence key is your account — there is no password. Polar holds it for you: open the {{ $mode }} customer portal and ask for a sign-in link by email.

  2. Sign in from the terminal

    Paste the key when prompted, or pipe it in: kwik cloud login --key-stdin. Then kwik cloud credits shows your balance and kwik cloud status the endpoint you are talking to.

Balance and job history are read with the key against the API. A browser view of the same numbers is not built yet: the Worker sends no CORS headers, so a page on this domain cannot call it. See the issue tracker.
